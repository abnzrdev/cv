Record · Review · Practise

From real conversation to useful practice.

The local research prototype combines speech transcription and speaker diarization with a review workflow for learners and teachers.

01 / RECORD

Capture a conversation

Use a short recording from two speakers. The prototype transcribes speech and keeps each speaker's turns separate.

02 / REVIEW

Inspect the moments

See possible long pauses, fillers, and English–Kazakh switching. Listen again and accept, edit or reject model suggestions.

03 / PRACTISE

Build the next exercise

Turn reviewed moments into editable speaking prompts and export feedback for each student and the class.

Thoughtful limitations

Assistance, not a language grade.

This is an experimental learning tool. Automatic transcripts and detected events may be inaccurate, especially in mixed-language speech.

  • Supports English–Kazakh conversation exploration.
  • Uses existing speech recognition and speaker models.
  • Teacher or learner review remains essential.
  • Does not yet assess pronunciation or fluency scores.
Current availability

The working prototype runs on a restricted research server. Public processing, accounts, and a hosted demo are not available yet. This page is for project information and early-access interest only. No audio is uploaded here.

Source code: the application repository is private. There is no public source-code or installer link.
